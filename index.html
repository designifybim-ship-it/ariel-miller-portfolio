About me

I notice design everywhere: the menu on the table, the sign above the shop, the font on the flyer.

I'm Ariel, a 25-year-old designer from St. Michael, Barbados. I care about how colour, type and layout make people feel, and I'll try version after version until a design says exactly what a business means.

At Altus Regional Consulting Solutions I created social media content, branded communications and marketing collateral, managed website updates and owned the firm's visual identity across digital and print. Through my own studio, Designify, I've designed logos, menus, packaging and social campaigns for small businesses across Barbados, and I designed and built the studio's website myself.

I also bring a customer-facing background from contact-centre and retail roles, so I design with the customer in mind.
